# Nuytelab outcomes

- [x] **Signed-in open collaboration and research submissions:** Any signed-in Manus account can access and edit the shared lab, open drafts and raw source text, contribute pasted text or searchable PDF, text, and Markdown files, and add attributed source material; no invitation, email allowlist, or collaborator-management step is required. The browser extracts PDF text, each contribution records the verified member, source title, optional citation URL, and content, and multiple contributions remain traceable. Public visitors can browse and review published papers but cannot read private drafts or source text. The protected owner Manus email retains the sole admin role for direct Notion publishing.
- [x] **AI-assisted synthesis and paper revision workflow:** The app generates a structured summarized research paper from the submitted source bundle, preserves source citation IDs and links, allows an editor to review and change the synthesis before release, and stores numbered revisions with an editor and change note.
- [x] **Direct Notion publishing:** The protected Notion integration publishes each approved non-demo paper version as a row with `Name` (title), `Date` (release date), `Summary` (full AI synthesis in rich text), and `Review` (concise synthesis). The connected table view places `Summary` immediately after `Date`. The row page retains the full summary and citations, and the Notion URL is saved to version history; until credentials are configured in a fresh deployment, the app offers Markdown export and does not claim a sync.
- [x] **Readable research-paper pages:** Public paper pages let visitors read a paper summary, inspect source citations and contributor attribution, and navigate revision history in a GitHub-inspired workflow; fictional seed material is clearly marked as demo content.
- [x] **Paper-grounded visitor Q&A:** Visitors can ask questions through the paper-specific chat panel, which answers with the published paper's synthesis and submitted sources as context, uses source labels where possible, and acknowledges missing evidence rather than inventing an answer.
- [x] **Community discovery, ratings, and reviews:** People can search community research by keyword, browse topic filters, open paper pages, rate papers on a 1–5 scale, and leave written reviews.
